# Limitations and responsible use

- The historical 0.705669 output is not claimed as an end-to-end clean
  regeneration because of its preserved table ancestry.
- Results depend on hosted Gemini APIs and can vary as the service changes.
- Corpus construction depends on publisher availability and PDF extraction
  quality. Scanned papers and complex tables remain difficult.
- The source-object indexes occupy several gigabytes and full corpus building
  is CPU-, storage-, and network-intensive.
- Source locators identify supporting regions; they do not prove that every
  generated interpretation is correct.
- Benchmark annotations are non-commercially licensed, and source PDFs retain
  their publishers' terms.
- TRACE should not be used to make high-stakes decisions without independent
  verification of both the cited paper and the generated answer.
